Peanut Clip Uploader

A private, single-user desktop tool (Google sign-in name: Peanut Factory) that uploads and schedules its owner's own edited videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, and to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.
