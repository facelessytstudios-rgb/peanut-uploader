Privacy Policy

App: Peanut Clips Uploader (a private, single-user publishing tool)
Last updated: 24 November 2026
Contact: facelessyt.studios@gmail.com

What the app does

Peanut Clips Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload short videos, which the owner edited, to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

  • With your permission (TikTok Login Kit), basic profile information (user.info.basic: display name, avatar, open ID) to show which account is connected.
  • Upload / publish permissions (video.upload, video.publish) to send the owner's own videos to their TikTok drafts or post them.
  • Creator info returned by TikTok (allowed privacy options, maximum video length) to choose valid settings for each upload.

How data is stored

Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own Windows computer, in Windows Credential Manager. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's API. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics outside TikTok.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the app. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (wording may vary), after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the computer.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.
