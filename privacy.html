Privacy Policy

App: Peanut Clip Uploader (Google sign-in name: Peanut Factory), a private, single-user publishing tool
Last updated: 25 November 2026
Contact: facelessyt.studios@gmail.com

What the app does

Peanut Clip Uploader is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to upload videos that the owner edited to the owner's own YouTube channel (through the YouTube API Services) and to the owner's own TikTok account (through TikTok's official Content Posting API). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app:

  • uploads the owner's videos to the owner's channel and sets their title, description, tags, privacy status, scheduled publish time and thumbnail;
  • reads the owner's channel ID and uploads list, only to confirm the correct channel is connected and to avoid uploading the same video twice;
  • reads back the status of each uploaded video (processing, privacy, scheduled time);
  • reads basic statistics of the owner's own videos (views, likes) to plan future uploads.

The app does not access, store or share any other user's data or any other channel's data.

TikTok

  • With the owner's permission (TikTok Login Kit), basic profile information (user.info.basic: display name, avatar, open ID) to show which account is connected.
  • Upload / publish permissions (video.upload, video.publish) to send the owner's own videos to their TikTok drafts or post them.
  • Creator info returned by TikTok (allowed privacy options, maximum video length) to choose valid settings for each upload.

How data is stored and shared

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own Windows computer, in Windows Credential Manager. Video statistics are kept in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the YouTube and TikTok APIs. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app uses no cookies and no tracking.

Retention, deletion and revoking access

Tokens and statistics are kept until the owner disconnects the app; the owner can delete them at any time from the computer. Google/YouTube access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. TikTok access can be revoked in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (wording may vary). After revoking, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the computer.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.
